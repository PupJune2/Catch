Privacy policy

This policy applies to the website junify.live, the web app app.junify.live and the Catch app for Android. We process as little data as possible – Catch records what, where and when something happened on a line – never who. This is a translation; the German version is legally binding.

2. Hosting and server logs

The website, web app and server run on a server operated by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany (data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR). When you visit, the web server stores technically necessary log data: IP address, date and time, requested address, status code, browser identifier. Purpose: secure operation and preventing abuse. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Logs are deleted after 14 days at the latest.

3. Website junify.live

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising trackers and loads no third-party content; fonts are served from our own server. The live numbers shown are anonymous totals.

4. Account and sign-in (Firebase Authentication)

You need an account to report, confirm and use live tracking. Sign-in is provided by Firebase Authentication from Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Your e-mail address, a password hash, a display name and a user ID are processed. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (terms of use).

5. Data Catch stores on our server

  • Account: user ID, e-mail address, display name, language, time of registration and last use, and when you accepted the terms of use.
  • Tips (reports): kind (delay, cancellation, crowding, disruption or ticket check), line, direction, the two stops (incl. their coordinates), time, selected tags (e.g. “very full”). Other users see reports without names or accounts. Free text and photos are deliberately not possible.
  • Flags: if you flag a tip (“Report content”), we store the tip, your user ID, the reason you chose and the time, so that we can review the content and prevent abuse. Flags are deleted together with the tip or your account.
  • Feedback: if you send us feedback (app → Profile → Send feedback), we store the category, your text, the optional rating, app version, platform and language. If you are signed in, the feedback is linked to your account; we only see your e-mail address if you agree to a reply by e-mail. Please don’t include data about other people.
  • Confirmations: your vote (“still there” / “gone”) on a report.
  • Live tracking: line, time window and your section (stops) while tracking is active.
  • Commutes (commuter mode): if you save a route (e.g. “Home → Work, weekdays 7:30”), we store its name, start and destination – a stop or an address with coordinates, so e.g. your home or work address –, the weekdays, the time, the kinds of tips you chose and the reminder setting, plus the most recently computed connection and when we reminded you. This is the only way Catch can watch the route and remind you while the app is closed. For planning, start and destination are sent – without account data – to the timetable service (see section 7). At the times of your route Catch creates automatic live trackings for the lines of the connection. The route is deleted immediately when you delete it in the app (Profile → My commutes) or delete your account; the automatic trackings one day after they end.
  • Device token: an identifier of your device for push notifications.

The purpose is operating Catch (showing reports, computing statistics, sending alerts) and preventing abuse (e.g. limiting reports per account). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

Retention

  • Reports are deleted after 365 days.
  • Live trackings are deleted one day after they end.
  • Feedback is deleted after 12 months at the latest, usually earlier once it has been dealt with.
  • E-mails you send us, and our replies to them, are deleted after 12 months at the latest (see section 9).
  • If you delete your account (app → Profile → Delete account), your account, device tokens, trackings, commutes and votes are deleted immediately. Your reports remain anonymised (without any link to you) for the statistics, as does your feedback (without any link to you).
  • Backups are kept for 30 days.

Moderation, trust and security log

For moderation (e.g. reviewing flagged content, suspending accounts that break the rules) the operator can view accounts, tips and feedback in a protected admin area. Only the operator has access, secured with a second factor. Every action there and every sign-in attempt is logged with time and IP address and deleted after 180 days.

  • Trust level: from the confirmations, disputes and flags on your reports in the last 90 days, from hidden reports, the number of your reports and the age of your account, Catch automatically calculates a trust level (0–3). It is recalculated from this data when needed and not stored as a separate profile. On each report we only store the level at the time of reporting and whether live alerts are held back until the first confirmation; others only see “trusted” or “unconfirmed”, never your account. If reporting is limited for your account automatically, we store until when. What the level does is described in the terms of use, section 7.
  • Moderation decisions: if one of your tips is hidden or deleted, your account is suspended or your reporting is limited – by the operator or automatically – we store the measure, the reason, an optional note, whether the decision was automated, the details of the affected tip (kind, line, stops, time) and whether the notification was sent. These entries are deleted after one year; if you delete your account earlier, they remain without any link to you until then.
  • E-mail notification: we inform you about such decisions, with the reasons and how to object, at your account’s e-mail address (sent via Resend, see section 9). If you object, a person reviews the decision again.
  • Replies to feedback: if you agreed to a reply and we answer you, we store the reply together with your feedback; it is deleted with it.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (security, preventing abuse and reliable tips) and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (obligations under the Digital Services Act, in particular the statement of reasons under Art. 17 DSA).

6. Location

Catch asks for your location only when you use a “nearby” feature – e.g. when reporting (“Which line are you on?”) or for stops near you. The coordinate is then sent to our server, rounded to about 10 m and passed on to the timetable service (see section 7) to look up stops and departures. No location history is stored and the coordinate is not linked to your account. You can revoke location access at any time in your device settings. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent via the system prompt).

7. Timetable data

Our server requests stops, departures and connections from the open timetable service Transitous. Only the search request is transmitted (e.g. stop name, start/destination, possibly a rounded coordinate) – without your IP address or account data, because the request comes from our server.

Address search and saved places

When you search for an address or a place (e.g. “Weseler Straße 108”), the search text is passed via our server to the timetable service (see above). “My location” uses your position once for the search. Places you save as “Home”, “Work” or favourites, and recently searched places, stay only on your device and are not sent to us. Exception: the start and destination of a commute you save are stored on our server (see section 5).

Map

The map view loads map images (tiles) from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org, map data © OpenStreetMap contributors). For technical reasons the tile server receives your IP address and the map section shown, but no account or location data from us. Tips and stops on the map come from our server. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

8. Push notifications

For live alerts while the app is closed we use Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google Ireland Limited, see section 4). The device token and the content of the alert (e.g. “Delay on U8 between A and B”) are transmitted. You can turn push notifications off at any time in the app or in your system settings. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

9. E-mails (password, moderation, replies, e-mails to us)

When you request a new password, Firebase (see section 4) creates a one-time link. We send the e-mail with this link via the service Resend (Resend, Inc., USA). Only your e-mail address and the content of the e-mail are transmitted. The transfer to the USA is based on the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. If Resend is not set up, Firebase sends the e-mail directly. You set the new password on our page junify.live/reset-password; the password goes straight to Firebase and is not stored by us.

We also use Resend to send notifications about moderation decisions that concern you (see section 5) and – only if you agreed when sending feedback – our reply to your feedback. Your e-mail address and the content of the e-mail are transmitted, for a reply also your feedback as a quote. If you reply to one of these e-mails, we receive your message and your e-mail address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 17 DSA (statement of reasons) or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (answering your request).

E-mails to us: e-mails you send to an address at junify.live – for example a reply to one of our e-mails or a message to our contact address – are received via Resend (see above) and passed on to our server. We store them up to 12 months to handle your request: sender address and name, recipients, subject, the text of the message (as plain text), technical details (date, message IDs, results of the sender checks SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and the names, types and sizes of attachments. We do not store attachments themselves. If the sender address belongs to a Catch account, we link the e-mail to that account. If we reply, we store our reply together with your e-mail; it is deleted with it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (your request) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering inquiries, legitimate interest).

10. Donations via Ko-fi

On the website and in the app we link to our page on Ko-fi (ko-fi.com/catchline, operated by Ko-fi Labs Limited, United Kingdom). Ko-fi only opens in your browser when you tap the link; no data is sent to Ko-fi before that, and we do not embed any content from Ko-fi. If you donate, Ko-fi and the payment service you choose (e.g. PayPal or Stripe) process your payment data under their own privacy policies. We only receive the details Ko-fi shows to page owners (e.g. the amount, the name you entered and, if given, your message and e-mail address). We do not link these details to your Catch account and only use them to record the donation and, where applicable, to thank you; they are kept as long as tax rules require. Transfers to the United Kingdom are covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time. To do so, write to the e-mail address above. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the authority of your federal state.
