Features

Everything for a relaxed ride.

Live tips from other passengers and smart insights that think ahead – so you know what to expect before you board.

Live

Pick a line and time – or your whole connection. As soon as someone reports something on your route, you get a pop-up with sound.

  • You choose which kinds alert you
  • Push notifications, even when the app is closed
  • Highlighted when it’s on your section
How it works

Three steps. No surprises.

1

Search your trip

Enter start and destination. You’ll see your connections – and at a glance which lines have current tips.

2

Start live tracking

One tap on “Live tracking” and Catch keeps an eye on your route – from boarding to arrival. If something is reported, you’re alerted right away.

3

Share what you see

Delayed, packed or doors out of order? Report it with two taps – and help everyone who boards after you.

Fair & safe

Tips, not exposure.

Catch is about the ride, not about people. Tips only say what, where and when – never who.

No photos, no free text

Tips only contain the kind, line, stops, time and fixed tags like “very full”. People can’t be described or identified.

Privacy first

No location history, no ad trackers. Reports appear anonymously. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Moderated by the community

Others confirm or dispute tips, and anyone can flag content. False or inappropriate tips are hidden automatically, spam is limited.

Catch is here to make your ride easier – not to dodge fares. Please always travel with a valid ticket. The house rules are in our terms of use.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Catch cost anything?

No, Catch is free – no ads, no tracking. If you like it, you can support it on Ko-fi.

What can I report?

Five kinds of tips: delays, cancellations (including replacement services), crowding, disruptions such as broken doors, heating or lifts – and ticket checks. Always with line, stops and time, using fixed tags. There is no free text and no photos.

Which cities does Catch work in?

All across Germany – wherever there are buses and trains. Timetables come from open data sources (including real-time data where available). How many reports a line gets depends on how many people take part there.

Do I need an account?

Not for searching and browsing. For reporting, confirming and live tracking you do – it protects against spam and false reports. An e-mail address and a password are all you need.

Is there an iPhone app?

For now, use Catch in your iPhone’s browser: open app.junify.live and choose “Share → Add to Home Screen”. A native iOS app is planned.

How do I install the Android app?

Tap “Get the Android app” and open the file. Android asks once whether to allow apps from this source – confirm, done. Updates install the same way over the old version.

How reliable are the reports?

As good as the community. Every tip shows how old it is and how often it was confirmed. Tips disputed or flagged by several people are hidden. Catch can’t guarantee that tips are complete or correct and doesn’t replace official information from transport operators.

What happens to my data?

Only your account (e-mail, display name) and your tips are stored. Catch uses your location only at the moment you search, to find stops nearby. Details are in the privacy policy.
